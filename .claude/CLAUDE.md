# plan-flow

A Claude Code mod that draws the current plan inside Claude Code: a live plan tree above the prompt, and a card flowchart in a side drawer where each box expands to its tasks and checkpoints.

## Decisions

- 2026-11-04 — Built into Claude Code as a mod, not a separate browser window: "화면을 따로띄우기보다 아예 mods 같은걸로내장할수있으면 더 좋긴할것같고".
- 2026-11-04 — The repo never says where code or ideas came from: no source names in code, comments, docs or commit messages ("우리레포에는 어디서 가져왔는지에대한거 싹지워"). No code is copied from other projects; it is written here, so `LICENSE` carries only our own copyright.
- 2026-11-04 — Two views: the plan tree above the prompt while a plan runs, and the card flowchart in a side drawer opened with `/flow`.
- 2026-11-04 — The flow supports branches and joins (parallel steps), not only a straight line.
- 2026-11-04 — Three nested levels, all drawn: stages → tasks → checkpoints ("두개가 별개인게아니라 하이라키구조"). Above the prompt the tree shows the levels, folding completed subtrees when rows run short; the drawer shows every level as boxes, and expanding a box goes one level down.
- 2026-11-04 — Whether the plan appears on its own when it starts, or only after `/flow`, is a user setting.
- 2026-11-04 — The drawer's look is the card flowchart (picked from three mockups): rounded stage boxes joined by `▼`, splits and joins drawn as connectors. `DESIGN.md` holds the tokens; `docs/design-reference/terminal/` holds the target drawing.
- 2026-11-04 — Parallel work is a `parallel` group inside the plan tree (series-parallel only), not free "depends on" links: it matches the card drawing and needs no graph layout engine.
- 2026-11-04 — Progress: one full plan send, then short updates by node id, plus a `next` op.
- 2026-11-04 — The plan comes only from the mod's own planner, never from plan-mode markdown or TodoWrite.
- 2026-11-04 — The board follows the session's work, not each prompt: "프롬프트 하나에 만드는게 아니라 작업의 설계를 잘해주고 관리해줘서 사용자가 해당 세션의 흐름을 항상 직관적으로 이해할수있도록". Continuing work adds steps to the current plan; a detour parks it and it comes back when the detour ends; starting over cancels it. A new plan over open work must say whether the old one is parked or cancelled.
- 2026-11-05 — The board is the plan of the work, not a log of what happened: the planner sees the stages with their ids, so continuing work and fixes land under the stage they continue, and a new task after a finished plan starts a new plan ("그냥 히스토리 목록처럼 아까전까지 뭐했고 지금 뭐하고있다 하면서 지금 카드, 마지막카드만 계속 바꾸는식으로 동작하는느낌이"). No warning for a plan past five stages.
- 2026-11-05 — A detour must not be appended as a new stage: the planner parks the plan with open work and brings it back when the detour ends ("detour 이거 데모에도있는데 (여기서도 제대로안됨) 제대로 있는기능맞냐").
- 2026-11-05 — The mod should leave the direction of the work and the rest of the harness alone as far as it can, ideally not touch them at all ("나는 이 플러그인이 작업의 진행방향이나 기존 하네스에 영향을 최대한 안줬으면해. 아예안줄수있으면 가장좋고"). Anything it adds to what Claude reads (system prompt, per-request notes, tool-answer hints, mid-work nudges) has to earn its place against a plugin-off run.
- 2026-11-05 — The board is kept by a small model the mod calls itself (Haiku), not by Claude: it looks when a request is sent, after every 6 state-changing calls, once a minute while Claude only reads, and when Claude answers, and answers with the board's ops. It hears every call, a subagent's included, with failed ones marked, and a notice (a task finishing) as a notice, never as the person's request ("진행중인걸 모른다거나 이런문제는없을까?", picked all three fixes). Nothing of plan-flow reaches Claude: no tool, no system prompt section, no note on requests or tool results, no refusals (picked "A", 옆에서 따로 그리기, after "아예안줄수있으면 가장좋고"). When Claude kept the board, a side-by-side run showed Haiku spending 7 board calls a session and 2.4× the time ("최근에 세션들의 작업이 뭔가 자동으로 잘 진행하지도 못하고 말귀도 좀 못알아듣는느낌인데"). The planner is told the ops' exact field names; without them it wrote `steps` for `nodes` and the board stayed empty.
- 2026-11-06 — Claude designs the plan, Haiku follows it: when Haiku starts a plan or changes its goal, a fork of the main conversation (no tools, answer kept by the mod) is asked once per request, after Claude has taken it, for the goal as the end the person is after and the stages the whole way there; Haiku keeps the stages ahead and tracks progress, hearing what Claude says between calls too ("써머리는 … 궁극적으로 하려는 무언가를 도출해낸느낌이아니라 현황 소개느낌이고, 여전히 단계도 이후 단계가 설계되어있지 않고", "응 그럼 너가 말한대로 해봐"). A fork per look would double the session's model use; a fork per plan costs about 3 to 5% of it. While Claude works, looks are at least 20 seconds apart, so twenty open sessions do not each call Haiku several times a minute ("난 세션을 보통 20개정도는 같이 켜두거든", picked "최소 20초 간격"); a request, an answer and a plan waiting for its design are looked at at once.
- 2026-11-04 — The planner parks or cancels on its own and never asks; unrelated work is parked like a detour.
- 2026-11-04 — Changes show as marks on steps (new, redo) and a parked list; a change log waits until it proves needed. The new mark is an inverted ` new ` badge ("새로 라고 하지말고 new 이런걸로하자. 그리고 일반 텍스트랑 구분이 잘안돼..").
- 2026-11-04 — Each step has a short title plus a one-sentence detail on what it does and why ("각 항목이 너무 축약되어있어서 뭘하고있는지 이해가잘안되는데.."). Above the prompt the detail shows under the running step; in the drawer, under open boxes and running steps.
- 2026-11-04 — Only two colours: green for done, red for blocked; everything else is plain text or muted ("색이 너무많아서 정신없어진것같다.. 녹색과 붉은색만 남기고 나머지는 흰색을써야하겠는데.."). No accent colour, no per-kind colours, save the band's `흐름도 보기`, a primary button in the surface's accent ("흐름도보기는 primary 처럼보이게하고 오른쪽에 붙", 2026-11-05).
- 2026-11-04 — The band above the prompt folds to its title and progress-bar lines ("접기도 있으면좋겠네 진행바행까지만 보이게"). Since 2026-11-05 the title itself folds and unfolds it, with only an arrow beside it and no fold button ("펼치기버튼을 따로둘지말고", "접기도 그냥 클릭하면 접히게하면 더좋고. 제목옆에 화살표만 넣어주고").
- 2026-11-04 — Steps carry links to what they produced (PR, issue, commit, artifact, file), shown in the drawer as one row of short names that open on click ("해당 단계에서 나온 아티팩트나 github url 등이 있으면 펼칠때 같이 항목아래 보여줄수있나? 근데 너무 복잡하지는않고 적당히 잘보이게"). The planner attaches only links that appear in the requests, call lines or answers it is handed.
- 2026-11-04 — Steps only the person can confirm get a "waiting for a check" status (`◐`) that counts as done, gathered on one "확인할 것 N" line under the progress bar; the plan holds no catch-all check step for the end ("내가 뭔가 해야하는데 블로커까진 아니고 이런요소들이 계속 남아서 진행율을 안높일것같은데 이거에 대해 좀 구조적으로 접근방식을 바뀌어야하지않을까").
- 2026-11-04 — The running step is drawn, not pointed at: a turning mark and a bright band sweeping its title while Claude works ("진행중인 항목에 그냥 화살표띡 하지말고 그래픽적으로 표현할방법있지않아?", picked from three mockups). The terminal redraws for each frame; the desktop app and editor get a picture that animates itself.
- 2026-11-04 — The band above the prompt has no scrolling and no "rows above/below" hint: at most 10 rows, a window around the current step, the rest in the drawer ("위 14줄 아래 1줄을 글로 보여줄 필요도 없을것같고 전체는 흐름도에서 이런소리도 보여줄필요없어보여").
- 2026-11-04 — The drawer pins its head (title, progress, steps to check, buttons to unfold and fold every box) and, when the body runs past the pane, a foot that follows the running step until the person scrolls; "진행 중으로" sits only in the foot ("1이긴한데 진행중으로가 중복되게 있는건 고쳐야할듯 아래에있는게맞을것같은", picked from three mockups). Finished stages start folded; boxes holding the running step or a step to check start open.
- 2026-11-04 — Finished steps step back dim (green `✓` kept), steps still to come read plain, the running step bold ("이미 완료된 항목과 진행중인 항목, 진행예정인 항목이 구분이 다 잘됬으면좋겠는데", picked from three mockups). The progress bar is one heavy line to half a cell, done in green over the same line dimmed ("아까 그냥 같은굴기선 반칸이 낫겠다 너무커진듯").
- 2026-11-04 — A drawer wide enough for the flowchart offers a "목록 보기" button to switch to the list a narrow drawer shows, and back ("서랍이 커져서 흐름도로 볼수있을때에 한정해서, 서랍이 작을때처럼 목록뷰로 토글하는 버튼이 보이면 좋을것같아").
- 2026-11-04 — The drawer's buttons have no hotkeys and are drawn as buttons (`[ 모두 펼치기 ]`) so they read as something to press ("단축키 없애자 잘 동작도안한다. 버튼이란거 알수있게나해줘").
- 2026-11-04 — A step pressed in the drawer offers "확인했어요" (on a step waiting for a check) and "질문", which fills the prompt box as a draft instead of sending ("확인했어요, 질문, 입력창에 초안채우기 가 좋겠다"). Changes the person makes on the board stay on the board; Claude is not told.
- 2026-11-04 — The view switch (목록 보기 / 흐름도 보기) sits flush right in the head so it stays put ("오른쪽으로 정렬시켜서 고정시켜. 위치가 바뀌니까 힘들다").
- 2026-11-04 — Flowchart boxes on one axis share one width, and side-by-side boxes split it evenly ("박스들의 크기가 다 제각각이니까 좀 힘든데 최소 너비를 정해주면 좀 보기편해지지않을까싶어", picked from two mockups).
- 2026-11-04 — Following keeps the running step in the middle of the drawer, with blank margin above and below the body so that holds near either end ("가운데로 두는건 좋은데 그럴수있으려면 상 하단에 패딩이있어야할듯"). "확인했어요" stays on steps waiting for a check only.
- 2026-11-04 — The step buttons read "확인 완료" and "논의": the second covers any talk about the step, not only questions ("질문은 질문만해야할것같아서 해당부분에 대해 이야기하는 모든 경우를 담는 버튼명을 골라야할것같은데", "'논의' 로 하자. 확인했어요는 '확인 완료'").
- 2026-11-04 — Added steps carry no mark; only a reopened step keeps its `↺` ("new 표시를 꼭 보여줄 필요는 없는게 맞는것같아"). This replaces the inverted ` new ` badge.
- 2026-11-04 — No elapsed times: neither the plan's total nor a step's own ("1시간 12분 이런 진행시간같은게 있는데 이거도 필요없는것같아"). A finished plan's "진행 중으로" goes to its last row ("다 끝났을때 진행중으로를 누르면 마지막 요소로 가야하는데").
- 2026-11-04 — A finished plan ends the drawer with a closing note on what the plan was for and how that was reached ("100%가 되면 어떤 목적이 어떻게 달성되었는지를 정리한 요소가 하나 맨밑에 나와야할것같아"). The planner writes it with the op that finishes the last step; it shows only at the end of the drawer, not above the prompt (picked from two options).
- 2026-11-05 — The closing note sets its labels (목적, 결과, 링크) in one column, wraps each text under its own start and leaves a blank row between parts ("완료정리 쪽이 좀 가시성을 좋게 열이나 단도 좀맞추고할수있나?", picked from three mockups).
- 2026-11-04 — Released as a public repo like review-inbox, with the screen and the README in English and Korean (picked from three options each). The language is the `language` option, else Claude Code's language setting, else the locale, else English.
- 2026-11-04 — Plans are built as a hierarchy: a few stages, each holding its tasks, never one flat list ("난 이게 적절하게 하이라키 구조가 되는걸 생각했는데, 그 구조화가 안되고 그냥 플랫 todolist 처럼 목록화해서 보여주고있어"). The planner's rules ask for 2 to 5 stages.
- 2026-11-05 — Steps are high-level checkpoints for following where the work stands, nested by the hierarchy, not code-level edits: no file names, code or commands in titles or details ("몇줄 수정하는지까지 다보고싶은건 아니야. 그 코드단위가 아닌 상위레벨의 상황파악을위한 체크포인트들이 하이라키구조에 맞게 나온다면 괜찮아"). Small edits are not forced into a plan.
- 2026-11-05 — Stages are named for what the task produces, never for the repo's routine (decision record, implement, PR): the routine goes inside the stage it serves ("결정기록, 구현, pr 같은 걸로만 묶여버리는데 너무 단순화되고 항상 똑같은게 나올까 걱정되거든?"). Grouping is measured with requests that carry an issue-to-PR routine, not only bare requests.
- 2026-11-05 — A newer version shows on the band's title line until the session reloads, compared against the copies installed beside this one and against the repository's manifest ("적절한 타이밍에 최신버전과 다른지 확인해서 reload-plugins 요청하는 기능도있어야할것같아"; picked "설치본 + GitHub" and "계획 띠에 한 줄"). No plan, no notice.
- 2026-11-05 — Each plan carries a goal, one sentence on what the session's work is for, under the title: one dim row in the band (kept when folded), up to three in the drawer's head ("이 세션이 뭘하는 세션인지에 대해 요약을 계획 최상위 영역에 써머리해주는게 있으면좋지않을까", picked "A + C" from three mockups). The planner writes it with op set and rewrites it with op update when the work widens.
- 2026-11-06 — The plan's title and goal name the work by its subject (what the change does), never "this", "current" or a bare number, so a glance while switching tabs tells which session it is ("내가 탭전환하다가 이걸보면 그냥 '현재 PR' 혹은 번호로만 써있는걸 알아먹고 아 이세션은 무슨 작업을 하는 세션이구나 하고 알수있을것같냐?", picked "규칙 + 제목 갱신"). The planner renames the plan with op update once the work shows its subject.
- 2026-11-04 — No line under the prompt: the band above it already shows the plan ("프롬프트 밑에 plan-flow 문구는 없어도될것같은데 프롬프트 위에 더좋은게 이미있잖아").
- 2026-11-05 — The band sits above review-inbox's, in the terminal and the desktop app alike, and `흐름도 보기` is framed like the drawer's buttons, as a primary button at the right edge ("cli 에서도 gui 에서도 plan-flow 가 review-inbox 보다 먼저 보였으면 좋겠고, 버튼이 버튼처럼 안보이는것같아"). The mod API has no order between plugins, so review-inbox draws what the plugins beneath it draw above its own rows; a test there holds it.
- 2026-11-05 — A step does not hold several things the person checks one by one: the planner's rules ask for one checkpoint per thing the person checks on its own ("지금하는 작업의 체크포인트들이 나눠지지않고 그냥 박스하나로 퉁치는 느낌", "이런일이 안일어나도록 plan flow 플러그인차원에서 관리해야하는거아니냐").
- 2026-11-05 — On the desktop app and the editor the drawer draws the flowchart as real boxes: section cards with A's lines between them, picked from four mockups ("c디자인에 a의 선이 있는게 좋아"); the terminal keeps the character flowchart. The app scrolls these cards itself, so following gives way to the focus on the step Claude is on.
- 2026-11-05 — The desktop app and the editor draw the band and the drawer in the app's own type, not monospaced character lines: a heading title, footnote-size details and counts, a progress bar picture, small rounded tags, native buttons, and an indented list instead of `├─` guides ("다 최대한 제대로구현해.. 프로덕트수준되게"). The terminal keeps its character drawing; both draw the same rows. The drawer's `목록 보기` stays the character tree there too, since its guides show the levels better than an app-type list ("목록 보기가 이상해진것같아. 기존보다 별로야", picked "이전 글자 트리로").
- 2026-11-05 — The desktop cards follow mockup C closely: a faint fill under each card's title row, hairlines between rows, a row that lights under the pointer, links at the row's end, done steps muted and only running ones semibold ("흐름도 gui 쪽이 시안에 비해 정말 한참모자라거든?"). Fills and edges are one neutral grey at low opacity, which reads on both themes where no theme key does.
- 2026-11-05 — On the desktop app every pressable row of the drawer, and the band's title row, is drawn as a Client so a press anywhere on the row counts ("계획의 문구부분을 정확히 클릭해야 인터렉션이 되는게 굉장히 불편해", "그냥 client 로 만들어 죄다"). Pictures (lines, progress bar, turning mark) stay outside them, since a Client cannot draw an Svg. A drawer too narrow for the flowchart shows the list there too ("gui 에서도 일정 너비이하일땐 목록보기로보여야해"), and a step's buttons are primary, at the right, apart from the text ("논의버튼도 좀더 primary 처럼").
- 2026-11-06 — `↺` marks a reopened step only while it is open, and a step that is done drops its note ("이마크는 왜보이는지도모르겠고", picked "열려 있을 때만" and "완료되면 지우기").
- 2026-11-06 — Work likely to follow the plan hangs dotted and dim after its last stage, two at most, never counted; it becomes a step when it starts and stays after the plan finishes ("높은가능성으로 예상되는 이후 작업들이 있으면 그것들도 점선으로 추가해주는건어떨까?", picked "1안 · 끝에 점선 꼬리" from three mockups; "끝난계획에도 남겨야할것같아"). Dotted is only work that waits on the person's go-ahead or on someone else; work Claude goes on with without asking, the routine after the request included, is a solid step ("요청한 일에서 파생된 너가 정말 세워서 나한테 묻지않고 진행할 계획들은 실선으로그려져야하는데..", 2026-11-06). The desktop app draws the line to it dotted and the card faint, since a Box border has no dotted style there.
- 2026-11-06 — A finished plan stays above the prompt, folded to its title, goal and progress, until the next plan starts; a press on the title opens it ("계획이 완료되면 입력창 위 ui 가 없어지는데 이거 별론것같아", picked "접어서 남기기").
- 2026-11-06 — A third colour, Claude Code's permission colour, means Claude waits on the person: a question or permission is open, or its turn ended with work open and no background work (agents, workflows, shells) still running; while that work runs the line reads `백그라운드 작업 N개 진행 중` in plain text ("workflows 가 돌거나 서브에이전트가 돌거나하는등 뭔가 답변을 실제로 기다리지 않는 상황에서도 답변기다리는중이라고 뜨는경우들이있던데", 2026-11-06). The line under the progress bar reads `답변 기다리는 중` and the running step's mark and `답변 기다림` take it ("다른색 하나 더쓰면어떨까", picked "A", 권한 확인 창의 파란 보라색). This widens the two-colour rule of 2026-11-04.
