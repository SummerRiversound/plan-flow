# Paseo look

The Paseo plugin's plan panel, picked on 2026-11-27 from three mockups (card flowchart, section list, vertical timeline): the card flowchart, the drawer's look of 2026-11-04 drawn as Paseo draws its own panels. Tokens and components are in `paseo/DESIGN.md`.

## Rules

1. Stages are cards on one axis joined by a thin stem; stages that run together sit side by side under a bracket, or stack under `동시에` on a narrow panel or past three.
2. The stage Claude is on opens on its own and finished stages stay folded, as in the mod; a press folds or unfolds and the tab remembers it.
3. Paseo's way of drawing a panel: bordered rounded cards on surface colours, the status icon on the left, counts flush right, muted second lines, chevrons on what folds.
4. Paseo theme keys only: success for done, danger for blocked, accent while Claude waits on the person. The running step stands out by weight and a turning mark, not colour.
5. The head says what is happening now from the agent's own state, not from the plan alone.
6. Above the composer, one pill `step · 3/13`; on the workspace row, one `3/13` badge. Both vanish when there is no open plan or the read failed.
7. No sections for questions, stuck work or activity, no time estimates and no left-to-right canvas: the panel is narrow and shows the plan only.
